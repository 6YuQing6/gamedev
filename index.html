EpiEat · 2026

Winner of Best Use of BackBoard for HackDavis, a two day UC Davis Hackathon. This is a mobile app that scans and translates restaurant menus and detects potential allergens, targetted towards foreign travelors and immigrants who can't read local menus or communicate their dietary restrictions. This was a collaboration between three people, for my part I designed the end-to-end UI/UX in Figma, implemented the interface using Replit, and directed the demo video.
