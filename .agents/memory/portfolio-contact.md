---
name: Portfolio contact
description: User's preferred contact interaction and why mailto was replaced.
---

The user wants Contact Me to copy their email and transition the plus sign to a checkmark, rather than launch an email application.

**Why:** The mailto link did not open anything on their computer, and they were concerned recruiters would encounter the same issue.

**How to apply:** Preserve the copy-email interaction when redesigning the contact button; do not restore mailto as its primary action. Show confirmation only after copying succeeds, with a selectable address when browser clipboard access is blocked.
